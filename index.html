A donation box asks people to give.
A Plinko kiosk gives them a reason to stop first.

How it works

Three seconds from curious to donor.

There is no interface to learn and nothing to install. The mechanism is gravity, and it explains itself the moment somebody else uses it.

  1. 01

    Drop the coin

    A clear, obvious slot removes any confusion about how to give. No app, no card reader, no queue.

  2. 02

    Watch it fall

    The coin bounces through the pegs in full view behind acrylic. That visible moment is what draws the next person over.

  3. 03

    Back your cause

    Your artwork and message surround the whole interaction, so every drop reinforces what the money supports.

Questions

The things people ask first.

What kind of coins does it take?

The kiosks are built around coin drop, so quarters and other standard coins work. If your site needs something different, tell us on the quote request and we will confirm what fits.

Can it live outside?

Yes. The freestanding model is weather-tested steel and high-thickness acrylic, built for all-weather use, with cooling and sensor options where appropriate to the site.

How much can we customize?

All four sides and the top. You can supply artwork or work with us on a direction. Graphics are interchangeable, so a seasonal campaign does not require a new machine.

Is it a lot of maintenance?

It is designed to reduce the repair and jamming problems found in standard donation kiosks. The mechanism is gravity and a fixed peg field, with no software to update and no consumables.

How long does a build take?

Every unit is built to order, so lead time depends on the model, the finish and the artwork. Send us the site and the message and we will give you a real date with the quote.
